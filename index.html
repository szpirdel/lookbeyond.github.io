Where the data comes from

Licensed sources, on the record

Discovery tools are only as defensible as the corpus underneath them. Ours is built licence first: nothing is downloaded until the terms are on file, and every document keeps the licence it arrived under.

Checked before it is fetched

Each source's licence page is captured with a date and a checksum, and the importer refuses to run without it. When the wording later changes, we are told.

Filtered record by record

Open access is not one thing. Records that forbid commercial use or derivative work are skipped individually, not waved through because the aggregator looked permissive.

Traceable back to the origin

Every document carries its source, its licence and its link, so any claim you build on a route can be followed back to where it came from.

The story behind it

Why I'm building this

The most valuable insights often live at the intersections between existing documents, papers, and articles — not in any single source. Today, that kind of discovery is reserved for well-funded institutions with large research teams. I want to change that.

LookBeyond is built on one simple conviction: knowledge discovery shouldn't depend on what you already know to look for. Whether you're tracking geopolitical events, researching across disciplines, or investigating complex networks of actors — the connections are already there. You just need the right tool to see them.
